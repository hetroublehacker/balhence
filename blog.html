About these guides

Advice you can put to work.

We write for people choosing and using a pentest: founders comparing quotes, engineers preparing access, and teams deciding which fixes to prioritize.

We distinguish technical references from our own judgment. External statistics include a source, date, context, and limitations. A pentest tells you about the systems and period tested; it cannot guarantee that a system is secure.
