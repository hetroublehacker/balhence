About the writing

For the person doing the work.

You might be booking your first pentest or preparing your fifth. These guides focus on the details that make the job easier: clear scope, working access, and a report your team can use.

Examples are illustrative. External research is linked and kept separate from claims about our work. No guide or time-limited test can promise a permanently secure product.
