Pricing FAQ

Before you compare proposals

Use the same scope, access model, reporting depth and re-test terms when comparing one price with another.

Are these fixed penetration testing prices?

They are starting prices for the scope shown. After a short scope review, we provide a written fixed-price proposal before testing begins. If your project fits the stated boundary, the starting figure is the expected entry point rather than a teaser for an undefined service.

What does every penetration test include?

Every assessment includes a written scope and rules of engagement, human-led testing, human-verified findings, urgent notification for confirmed critical issues, a technical report, a findings walkthrough and one bounded re-test of original findings within 30 days unless the proposal states otherwise.

What can change the starting price?

Additional applications, environments, roles, tenants, integrations, cloud accounts, live hosts, source review, restricted windows, urgency and specialist evidence needs can change the fee. We explain each change in the proposal.

Are taxes included?

No. Published prices are in US dollars and exclude applicable taxes and agreed third-party costs. Your proposal states the invoice currency, taxes and payment terms.

Can I buy an automated scan for less?

These prices are for penetration testing with human investigation and validation, not a scanner export. If you need a narrower release question, the focused check is the smallest starting scope.

Does the report guarantee certification or a vulnerability-free system?

No. The report records the tested scope, version, methods, findings and limitations. Audit acceptance belongs to the responsible auditor, and no point-in-time assessment can guarantee that a system is vulnerability-free.
