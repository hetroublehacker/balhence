Planning assumptions & exclusions

Assumptions

  • Written authorization and rules of engagement are agreed before testing.
  • Controlled accounts and safe test data are supplied.
  • Critical findings use an agreed escalation path.

Always excluded unless explicitly agreed

  • Denial of service or destructive testing
  • Social engineering and physical security
  • Access to real customer data beyond minimum safe proof
  • Assets and environments outside the signed scope
