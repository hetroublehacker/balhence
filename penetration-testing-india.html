Clarify the work behind VAPT

Vulnerability assessment or penetration testing?

VAPT is often used as a combined label. Ask whether a proposal includes discovery, manual validation, authenticated workflows, and a re-test. These are separate coverage decisions, and the abbreviation alone does not define them.

Choose the evidence your team needs
AssessmentUseful questionConfirm in the proposal
Vulnerability assessmentWhich potential weaknesses need review and prioritization?Asset coverage, access level, validation effort and how uncertain findings are labelled.
Manual penetration testingCan a weakness affect access, data or an important business workflow?Roles, tenants, workflow coverage, safe impact evidence and exclusions.
Remediation re-testDid the agreed fixes address the reported issues?Finding IDs, changed version, checks repeated, outcome and anything not re-tested.

From brief to handover

How remote penetration testing in India works

The project starts with a written scope, not a scan. Testing begins only after the system owner has authorized it and the safety rules are agreed.

1. Define the boundary

Share the systems, environments, roles, integrations and reason for testing. We agree on exclusions, test accounts, data handling and what the report needs to answer.

2. Coordinate remote access

We set a testing window with your team, including the time zone, monitoring contact and escalation path. Production testing needs explicit safety limits.

3. Test and validate

Manual work focuses on access rules and important workflows. We verify reported findings, describe their effect and flag urgent issues through the agreed channel.

4. Explain the results

You receive reproducible findings, risk context and repair guidance. We walk the team through the results; any re-test is agreed in the scope.

Working across India

What should your India VAPT brief include?

Use this short brief to make a remote test easier to scope and compare:

  • Systems and access: applications, APIs, environments, user roles and controlled test tenants.
  • Timing: the testing window in India Standard Time (IST), a monitoring contact and an urgent-findings channel.
  • Data rules: where evidence may be stored or processed and any restrictions on tools or AI use.
  • Review deadline: who needs the report, their required format and the date they need it.
  • Follow-up: whether a walkthrough or re-test is required and when fixes can be checked.

What should a VAPT finding prove?

Our fictional sample uses two controlled test tenants to show the evidence behind an access-control finding. It illustrates a report format, not a client result.

Reproduce the boundary failure

A standard user in synthetic Tenant A requests an invoice owned by controlled Tenant B by changing only the object ID on GET /api/v1/invoices/{id}.

Show the effect

The fictional response returns Tenant B's invoice instead of denying access. The finding records the test accounts, request, response and potential cross-tenant data exposure.

Define the fix and re-test

Constrain invoice lookup to the authorized tenant. Then check that cross-tenant reads and exports are denied while the same-tenant read still works; record any routes outside the re-test scope.

Compare proposals

How to compare VAPT providers and proposals in India

When comparing penetration testing quotes in India, ask what is actually tested and what your team receives. A lower number is hard to compare until the scope is clear.

01 / COVERAGE

Check the test boundary

Does the proposal name the applications, APIs, environments, roles, tenant relationships and critical workflows? Ask which third-party assets and risky techniques are excluded.

02 / EVIDENCE

Ask to see the report format

Each finding should show what was tested, how it can be reproduced, its business effect and a practical fix. Ask how findings are checked before they enter the report.

03 / HANDOVER

Confirm what follows

Compare the testing window, urgent-issue escalation, engineer walkthrough and re-test terms. If a customer or auditor will read the result, say so before the work begins.

Before you request a quote

Penetration testing in India: common questions

Share the product and deadline first. The scope determines the effort and deliverables.

Is this a VAPT service for companies in India?

Yes. For web, API and SaaS products, we plan an authorized penetration test with your team and deliver it remotely. The proposal defines the exact systems, approach and report.

How is the price determined?

It depends on the number of systems, user roles, integrations, environments and depth of testing. Tell us your goals and timeline; we will write a scope and quote before any testing starts. The cost and scope guide explains the main effort drivers.

Can the report support a customer or audit review?

Share the reviewer's exact requirements, including any named assessor or report format. We will check whether the proposed scope and deliverables fit before starting. A pentest alone does not certify compliance or guarantee audit acceptance.

Do we need a CERT-In empanelled auditor?

Check the exact customer, tender or regulatory requirement. If it names an empanelled auditor, verify the proposed provider against CERT-In's current list before commissioning the work. This page does not claim CERT-In empanelment; a general VAPT report should not be presented as an empanelled audit.

Can you test our live product?

When authorized and appropriate, yes. We agree on safe techniques, test accounts, rate limits, escalation contacts and stop conditions first. Higher-risk scenarios may need a representative test environment.
