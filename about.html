About Balhence

Start with the problem.
Connect the right skills.

Balhence is an AI-native security practice. We bring research, testing, code review, and defensive engineering together to help you understand risk and make your systems stronger.

The scope can span web and mobile applications, APIs, cloud, infrastructure, identity, AI systems, source code, and detection. The important part is how they connect: a product, its permissions, the services behind it, and the people responsible for it.

AI is part of how we research, analyze, and build. We use it to connect information and develop workflows and custom defensive tools around your needs. People set the direction, check the evidence, and stay accountable for the outcome.

Bring us the challenge, even if it doesn't fit a service label. We'll work out the right mix of skills, access, deliverables, and specialist support, then agree on a scope we can stand behind.

What you can expect

Four commitments for every engagement.

01 / SCOPE

Agree before we act.

We confirm the outcome, systems, access, methods, data handling, contacts, and stop conditions before work begins.

02 / PROOF

Make the result verifiable.

AI output is a starting point, not proof. People validate findings, review changes, and check results against the agreed goal.

03 / CARE

Keep the impact small.

We use controlled accounts, synthetic data, a representative record, or another limited method agreed in the testing rules.

04 / HANDOFF

Leave you ready to use it.

You get clear findings or working deliverables, a walkthrough, and the validation or re-testing agreed in your scope.

AI-native, by design

AI in the workflow.
People in charge.

AI is part of the process from research to delivery, not just a final editing step. We use it where it helps, within your approved data-handling rules, and validate what comes out.

Where AI fits

  • Researching technologies and organizing approved project context
  • Connecting observations across code, configuration, and workflows
  • Helping review threats, controls, and detection coverage
  • Developing custom defensive tools and repeatable checks
  • Organizing redacted evidence with traceable sources
  • Drafting documentation, remediation options, and handover notes

What people own

  • The scope, permissions, and safety of each action
  • Architecture decisions and the tradeoffs behind them
  • Validation of findings and review of generated code
  • The meaning of the evidence and its business impact
  • Recommendations, priorities, and quality of delivery
  • Checking fixes and deliverables against the agreed requirements
Your data and AI

AI use is documented in the scope and rules of engagement. No public model receives client credentials, tokens, customer records, source code, or unredacted vulnerability evidence unless the client explicitly approves the provider, purpose, and processing terms. No model autonomously tests production.

Balhence / Santulan

Security has to work with your product.

The name Balhence comes from santulan, meaning balance. Your team has releases to ship, customers to support, and data to protect. Security advice needs to account for all of that.

We connect security work to what your team is trying to do: ship a product, adopt AI, strengthen infrastructure, or build a better way to detect problems. The result should help you move forward with a clear view of the tradeoffs.

Before you choose

Clear expectations

  • The work covers the systems and outcomes we agree on
  • Results reflect the version and access available during the work
