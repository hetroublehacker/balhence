How this archive is published

The nine 2026 field notes retell controlled private research in a first-person format. Organizations, products, routes, identifiers, dates, and UI labels are removed or replaced; examples are synthetic, and no repair, retest, or program acceptance is claimed. The ten articles from 2019 to 2022 preserve their original wording and publication dates, so some tools and links reflect their historical context.
