How we work

Tools help. We check the work.

We're AI-native: approved AI tools help with research, analysis, and engineering. A person validates findings and takes responsibility for delivery. Here's how we use AI in testing.

Start with the real question

What are you building, changing, or worried about? That conversation sets the direction before we pick tools or write a test plan.

Bring the right skills

We match the work to the systems involved and confirm specialist requirements early. You know what is covered and where the limits are.

Agree on AI and data use

AI workflows, approved tools, and data handling are part of the engagement agreement. Sensitive material stays within the agreed boundaries.

Keep people in control

Tools support the work; they do not expand the authorization. Human review and agreed safety controls guide consequential actions.

Check the evidence

Reported findings are human-verified. We distinguish confirmed issues from open questions and explain their practical impact.

Make the next step clear

You get useful findings, priorities, and recommendations. Implementation support and follow-up validation can be included in the scope.

Common questions

A few things to know.

What's included, how testing starts, and what happens if we find something urgent.

Are you only a web and API pentesting company?

No. Application testing is one part of the practice. We also scope cloud, infrastructure, identity, AI, code, secure delivery, detection, readiness, and specialist work. You can combine capabilities in one engagement.

What does AI-native mean for my project?

We use approved AI assistance to support analysis, review, research, and tooling where it is useful. People remain responsible for decisions and validate reported findings. We agree on tool use and data handling before sharing engagement material with AI services.

Can I bring a project that is not listed?

Yes. Tell us what you're trying to work out. We'll discuss whether we can help, what skills and access it needs, and a first milestone. Specialist work depends on confirmed expertise and safe testing conditions.

Can you test production?

Yes, when production testing is authorized and appropriate. The rules of engagement must define safe techniques, rate limits, test data, customer-data handling, monitoring, escalation contacts, and stop conditions. A representative staging environment is preferred for riskier scenarios.

Can you help with a customer review or audit?

Share the request, framework, and deadline during scoping. We can help organize evidence and identify technical gaps, while being clear about what needs a formal auditor or certification body. An assessment does not guarantee certification or audit acceptance.

Do you provide a clean letter after remediation?

The re-test update records each finding as fixed, partially fixed, not fixed, or not re-tested. A concise closure letter can summarize the agreed outcome without claiming that the entire system is vulnerability-free.

Can we start before procurement is complete?

No testing begins without an executed agreement, written authorization, finalized scope, and rules of engagement. Scoping and document review can happen while procurement progresses.

What if a critical issue appears during testing?

We use the agreed escalation channel immediately rather than waiting for the final report. Testing pauses if the rules or safety conditions require it.
