04 / Good questions

Before we
get started.

Send us a question
What does AI-native mean here?

AI is built into how we research, analyze information, develop tools, and prepare evidence. We agree on its use and data handling with you. People remain responsible for testing decisions, verified findings, and the final work.

Do you work beyond web and API testing?

Yes. Our services cover applications, cloud, infrastructure, identity, AI systems, source code, defensive automation, detection, readiness, and specialist systems. Projects can combine disciplines instead of fitting a fixed package.

What if our project is unusual?

Bring us the challenge. We start with discovery to confirm the expertise, environment, permissions, and resources it needs. If specialist support or a different approach is needed, we'll say so before committing.

What does the engagement include?

That depends on the outcome: an assessment, security engineering, research, or readiness work. Your proposal sets out the deliverables, dates, exclusions, and price. Our web and API pentest packages include a report, walkthrough, and one agreed re-test.

Can you work with our deadline?

Tell us when you need the work delivered and what your customer or team has asked for. We will check the work involved and our availability before confirming dates.

Can we see a report before talking?

Yes. The interactive sample report uses fictional examples to show the detail you can expect, including how we record a re-test. You can also download the sample PDF.
